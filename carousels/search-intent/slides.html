01
What is it?

Search intent = the “why”

It's the reason behind a search: what the person actually wants to do when they type it.

Same topic, different goals
  • “cake recipe” → wants to bake at home
  • “best cake shop in Pune” → wants to compare options
  • “order eggless cake Pune” → wants to buy now
02
The 4 types

Every search has one of 4 intents

📚
Informational
Wants to learn
“how to bake eggless cake”
🧭
Navigational
Wants a site
“Monginis near me”
⚖️
Commercial
Wants to compare
“best cake shop in Pune”
🛒
Transactional
Wants to buy
“order birthday cake online Pune”
03
The trap

Why high volume misleads you

Volume tells you how many people search. It never tells you why.

Rank #1 for “cake recipe” and you get
  • Lots of traffic from home bakers
  • People who want a recipe, not a delivery
  • Zero orders, high bounce rate

Big numbers look great in a report. They don't pay the bills.

04
Why intent wins

Intent beats volume, every time

Target the right intent and you get
  • Higher conversions: visitors arrive ready to act
  • Easier rankings: specific keywords have less competition
  • Google's favour: it ranks pages that match intent
  • AI answers too: ChatGPT & AI Overviews also match the “why”

1,000 visitors who want to buy beat 1,00,000 who just want a recipe.
