01
Reason 1

AI can't verify your business.

✕The problem
Your name, address, phone or hours are different on Google, your website and listing sites. AI isn't sure the details are right, so it plays safe and names someone else.
✓The fix
Use the exact same name, address, phone and hours everywhere. Complete every field of your Google Business Profile.
2 / 5→
02
Reason 2

Your reviews don't say what you do.

AI reads review text to match you to what people ask.

★★★★★
“Great service! Highly recommend.”
Tells AI nothing
★★★★★
“Best root canal in Nashik. Painless and same-day.”
AI can match this
✓The fix
When you ask for a review, ask customers to mention the service they got and your area.
3 / 5→
03
Reason 3

Your website doesn't answer questions.

✕The problem
People ask AI full questions: “How much does it cost?”, “Do you deliver to my area?”, “Are you open Sunday?” If your site never answers them, AI quotes a site that does.
✓The fix
Add an FAQ page with the real questions customers ask you on calls and WhatsApp, answered in plain words.
