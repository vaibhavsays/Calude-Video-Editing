01
Strategy

Win on Bing SEO

ChatGPT often searches the live web through Bing. If Bing can't crawl and index you, ChatGPT can't find you.

For the Jaipur brand
  • Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap
  • Turn on IndexNow so new products get indexed fast
  • Fix slow pages, broken links and blocked product pages
02
Strategy

Earn third‑party citations

ChatGPT rarely trusts a brand that only praises itself. It looks for other trusted sites vouching for you.

For the Jaipur brand
  • Collect real reviews on Nykaa and Amazon.in
  • Pitch the founder story to YourStory, Inc42 and beauty blogs
  • Get listed on directories like JustDial and in “best of” roundups
03
Strategy

Show up on Reddit & forums

LLMs weigh real, transparent conversations where actual users discuss and review products.

For the Jaipur brand
  • Answer oily‑skin questions on r/IndianSkincareAddicts and Quora
  • Always say you're the founder. No fake accounts
  • Give genuinely useful routines, not just links
04
Strategy

Structure content for AI

Clear H1/H2/H3, a direct answer first, comparison tables. No fluff.

05
Strategy

Publish “Best for” guides

People ask AI “best [product] for [use case]”. Publish the page that answers it.

Best ayurvedic face wash for oily skin in India
Best face wash for acne under ₹500
Neem vs tea tree face wash: which is better?
Best skincare for humid Mumbai weather

Tip: be fair and include competitors. Honest guides get cited.
