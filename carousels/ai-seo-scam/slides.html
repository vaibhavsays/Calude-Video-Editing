6 red flags ahead
Swipe →
01
🚩 Red flag

Guaranteed AI rankings

RED FLAG
They say
Guaranteed #1 on ChatGPT in 30 days.
✓ Reality

No one controls what ChatGPT, Perplexity or AI Overviews say. They run fresh, real-time searches and never answer the same way twice.

Ask“What exactly is guaranteed, and what happens if it doesn’t?”
02
🚩 Red flag

AI SEO sold as separate magic

RED FLAG
They say
Forget SEO. You only need our AI package.
✓ Reality

AI engines pull answers from normal search indexes (Bing, Google). Without solid technical + content SEO, there is nothing for AI to find.

Ask“How does this connect to my existing SEO?”
03
🚩 Red flag

Tricks over real content

RED FLAG
They say
Urgent! We must edit robots.txt and ‘chunk’ your articles for AI.
✓ Reality

AI cites real, useful content and brands with authority: reviews, mentions, expert pages. Code tricks don’t replace that.

Ask“Which pages and content will you improve?”
04
🚩 Red flag

Fake AI dashboards

RED FLAG
They say
Your ChatGPT impressions this month: 12,480 📈
✓ Reality

Standard tools like Google Search Console don’t expose exact ChatGPT or AI Overview impressions. Numbers like this need a real source.

Ask“Where exactly does this data come from?”
05
🚩 Red flag

Vague buzzword answers

RED FLAG
They say
Our proprietary neural algorithm handles everything.
✓ Reality

Real experts explain in plain words how they track AI crawlers, fix server responses and build entity signals. Buzzwords hide nothing.

Ask“Explain your process step by step.”
06
🚩 Red flag

No proof you can check

RED FLAG
They say
Just trust our monthly report.
✓ Reality

Real progress shows in tools you own: Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools (with its Copilot AI performance report). Can’t verify it? Walk away.

Ask“Give me access to check this in my own accounts.”
