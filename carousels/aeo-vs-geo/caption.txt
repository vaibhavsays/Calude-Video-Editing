AEO vs GEO: same goal, different game 👇

Both help you show up when people ask AI. But they're not the same job.

🔵 AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)
Getting Google and voice assistants to use YOUR text as the direct answer: featured snippets, People Also Ask, Siri & Alexa.
→ It depends on how your page is written.

🟣 GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Getting ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews to mention, recommend or cite your brand.
→ It depends on what the whole web says about you.

The simple way to remember it:
AEO makes your pages easy to quote.
GEO makes AI trust you enough to quote you.

You need both. Start with AEO on your own site this week (question headings, 40–60 word answers, FAQ schema). GEO builds over months (mentions, reviews, your own data).
